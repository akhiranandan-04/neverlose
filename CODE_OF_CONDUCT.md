# Code of Conduct

This project exists to make documentation clearer. Participants are expected to
keep discussion focused, technical, and respectful.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique the work, not the person.
- Ask for evidence when a claim is uncertain.
- Respect privacy; do not repost deleted content or personal data.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.
- Accept a maintainer's decision to narrow scope or close an out-of-policy change.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when recommending a product or service.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Impersonating the vendor, a maintainer, or another contributor.
- Spam, paid promotion, fake engagement, or repeated off-topic links.
- Distributing malware, stolen credentials, or executable files.
- Pressuring contributors to provide account data or paid support.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project space
where someone represents this repository.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock a thread, or block an account when
behaviour breaks these rules. Enforcement is best-effort and proportional to the
problem.

Report a conduct problem privately to a maintainer through their GitHub profile.
Do not include account credentials or other sensitive data. Security issues are
handled separately under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
