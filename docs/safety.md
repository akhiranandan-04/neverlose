# Safety

This page is intentionally direct. A documentation repository can reduce
confusion, but it cannot make third-party software safe for your account or your
computer.

## Account risk

Using third-party software with CS:GO can violate Valve's Steam Subscriber
Agreement and can result in a VAC or game ban. Detection can happen without a
warning, and no guide can promise otherwise.

- Do not use an account you cannot afford to lose.
- Do not trust "undetected" claims from strangers.
- Do not pay anyone who promises to remove a VAC ban.
- Do not share Steam Guard codes, passwords, API keys, or session files.

If account safety is the priority, the reliable choice is not to load the
software at all.

## Download hygiene

- Use only the [download folder linked in the README](https://www.mediafire.com/folder/xpuwhpaw2jpbi/neverlose).
- Check the address before downloading. Lookalike domains and short links are
  common.
- Do not use reuploads, torrents, Telegram attachments, or files from direct
  messages.
- Keep security software enabled. Do not add exclusions just to make an archive
  open.
- If a file is flagged, stop. Verify the source instead of overriding the warning.
- If the folder publishes a checksum, compare it with the archive yourself.

This repository does not mirror the build and cannot verify files downloaded
from other places.

## Privacy

Public issues are public forever. Before posting:

- Remove your Steam name, account ID, email address, and local file paths.
- Remove tokens, cookies, session data, and configuration exports.
- Crop screenshots to the relevant area.
- Do not upload full logs unless you have reviewed every line.

If you accidentally publish a secret, revoke it first. Deleting the post is not
enough because the content may already be indexed or copied.

## What this repository will never ask for

- Your Steam password or Steam Guard code.
- Your Steam session or browser cookies.
- A paid "verification" or "unban" fee.
- A file transfer through a private message.
- Your account credentials for "testing".

Treat any message that asks for these as impersonation, even if it appears to
come from a contributor.

## Repository policy

- Documentation and original assets only.
- No executables, archives, loaders, DLLs, or dumped source.
- No account data, private configs, or leaked credentials.
- No mirror links, paid engagement, or fake activity.
- No instructions for evading anti-cheat, detection, or platform moderation.
- No harassment, impersonation, or vendor support claims.

## Reporting a problem

- Broken guide or link: open a documentation issue.
- Malicious link or leaked secret: follow [SECURITY](../SECURITY.md) and do not
  post the sensitive material publicly.
- Account ban: this hub cannot help. Do not post account details while asking.

## Platform availability

GitHub, Valve, and the vendor can change their policies or availability at any
time. This repository cannot guarantee that the project, the download folder, or
the game client remains online. Keep local copies of documentation you rely on.
